# Final rank frames

Built-in ImageGen edits of the original frames. Final artwork includes gold crown and baked rank digits 1, 2, and 3. The portrait holes and exterior remain transparent.

## frame-gold.png: crown and medallion

Use case: precise-object-edit
Edit target: supplied frame-gold.png, the circular polished antique gold and warm amber highlights portrait frame.
Primary request: Complete this leaderboard portrait frame with a round rank-number medallion attached at the bottom. Add a prominent beautiful gold five-point royal crown centered above the top rim, like a first-place champion portrait in a premium fantasy game. Crown has small amber jewels and clear silhouette.
Preserve the existing polished metal, delicate lower laurel leaves, circular frame style, and transparent portrait opening. Recompose slightly within the same square so no ornament clips: circular ring centered at 50% X and 50% Y, outer ring diameter approximately 76% of canvas; portrait opening diameter approximately 66% of canvas. All three frame variants need this same center and opening size.
Bottom addition: a single circular embossed polished antique gold and warm amber highlights medallion centered at 50% X and 86% Y, diameter 18% of canvas, overlapping the lower ring. It has a thick crisp metallic rim, small laurel details, a flat dark navy inset disk for an HTML rank numeral to be placed later. Leave disk completely BLANK: no text, no number.
Top addition: crown centered at 50% X and 11% Y, total width about 30% of canvas, above and attached to ring, safely inside image boundaries.
Constraints: outside transparent, large inner portrait hole genuinely fully transparent. Keep medallion navy disk opaque. No portrait, face, text, letters, numbers, watermark, checkerboard. ONE finished complete border only, centered front view. Preserve true alpha.
